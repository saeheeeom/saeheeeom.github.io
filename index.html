I study language models with a linguist's eye. Recently I've looked at where social reasoning comes from in pretraining data, using training-data attribution and machine unlearning in Georgia Tech's Human-Centered AI Lab with Prof. Mark Riedl and PhD student Glenn Matlin. I've also studied how LLM negotiators behave differently when the same persona speaks Korean instead of English.

Before grad school I studied AI and Linguistics at Seoul National University and worked in data science at Salesforce and LBOX. This summer I interned at GE Vernova. I graduate in December 2026 and am looking for full-time AI/ML engineer and data science roles.
