Hybrid vector search and RAG with query routing

2025

Georgia Tech, Database Systems (team project)

FAISS (IVF-PQ) vector search over sentence-transformer embeddings, combined with SQLite metadata filters over 150K Wikipedia articles and fed into a LangChain RAG pipeline. Four strategies (filter-first or search-first, fixed or adaptive) cut latency 3–30×. A 2D-histogram selectivity estimator and a logistic-regression router choose the strategy per query (~96% F1, under 0.5 ms).

2024

Adds imperceptible noise to personal photos so that DreamBooth fine-tuning on them fails to reproduce the person's identity. Fine-tuned DreamBooth with LoRA, compared background-only and full-image perturbation, and evaluated with FDFR, ISM, and SER-FIQ.
